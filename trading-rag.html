The problem

Search was not enough.

The archive mixes live commentary, later reviews, screenshots, trade outcomes, and concept explanations. A useful answer has to retrieve the right material while preserving what was known at the time, what was only learned later, and what remains uncertain.

The system therefore separates semantic authoring from deterministic retrieval infrastructure. Reviewed cards describe what happened and why; the retrieval layer validates, compiles, routes, retrieves, selects evidence, and constrains answer generation.

End-to-end architecture

From raw archive to grounded answer.

The semantic corpus is treated as a loss-preserving layer. Downstream retrieval and modeling can project from it, but do not silently rewrite the reviewed source interpretation.

Retrieval design

Semantic planning without giving the model hard control.

The system deliberately separates objective constraints from semantic hypotheses so that a planner mistake cannot silently erase otherwise eligible evidence.

Hard boundary

Deterministic scope

Explicit dates are resolved against dates actually present in the compiled corpus. Invalid or unavailable dates are rejected instead of guessed.

query → date resolver → eligible cards
Soft hypothesis

Semantic planner

The planner classifies retrieval, comparison, or supported aggregation; normalizes wording; adds curated expansion terms; and proposes preferred card types.

intent + operation + rewrite terms
Redundancy

Two lexical paths

The original query is always preserved. Raw and rewritten BM25 searches run independently, then deterministic weighted score fusion combines them.

rewritten0.65×
Failure-safe

Planner fallback

If the OpenRouter planner is unavailable or returns an invalid plan, the system falls back to a deterministic heuristic route rather than failing the query.

Grounding & temporal integrity

The answer is constrained after retrieval too.

01

Finite evidence IDs

Generation receives a bounded evidence bundle. Citations to unknown evidence are rejected.

02

Claim authority

Evidence types carry explicit authority such as historical event, concept authority, visual observation, or structured fact.

03

Support status

Answers are validated as supported, partial, or unsupported; unsupported outputs cannot quietly contain factual claims.

04

Bounded repair

A repair pass receives the same evidence plus exact validation failures. It cannot expand scope or add new evidence.

05

Graceful fallback

If model generation remains unavailable or invalid, an extractive fallback surfaces the strongest retrieved evidence instead of fabricating an answer.

Why chronology matters
10:17Live condition stated

What the trader could know and act on at that moment.

10:51Condition becomes active

A later event can update the decision state without rewriting the earlier one.

Later reviewHindsight explanation

Useful context, but not imported into the earlier live information state.

The corpus keeps event time, posted time, revealed time, evidence-first-available time, and review timestamp distinct when the source supports them.

Corpus rules

Designed to preserve uncertainty instead of cleaning it away.

Unknown ≠ false

Missing evidence stays unknown, unresolved, or missing-source rather than being converted into a negative fact.

Non-action is data

Waiting, skipping, invalidating, lowering confidence, and ending participation are first-class decisions.

Competing hypotheses stay

The day is not rewritten around the path that eventually happened; abandoned and unresolved branches remain visible.

Review ≠ live premise

Later explanations may clarify or criticize a decision, but cannot be inserted into what was knowable beforehand.

Verification

Built to be regression-tested.

The project includes unit, integration, and regression coverage across the validator, compiler, registry integrity, date isolation, query routing, BM25 retrieval, semantic reranking, evidence selection, grounded answers, answer repair, user-facing output, and structured aggregation.

Versioned official regression suites through v0.13 make architectural changes testable against earlier behavior rather than relying on a handful of demo queries.
